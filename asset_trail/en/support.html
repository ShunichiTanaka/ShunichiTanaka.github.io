Frequently asked questions

Where are my records stored?
Records are stored on your device. Export a JSON backup from Settings before changing devices or deleting the app.

How can I restore Plus?
Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then restore your purchase from the in-app purchase screen.
