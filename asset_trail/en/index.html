HOW IT WORKS

Seconds to log.
Easy to look back on.

No accounts to connect, no spreadsheets to maintain. Just enter the total you see today, and AssetTrail builds the bigger picture over time.

  1. Log your total

    Pick a date and enter your total assets. Add cash flow or a quick note if you like.

  2. Watch the trend

    Switch between line, weekly, and monthly charts to see the ups and downs as one clear path.

  3. Review with insights

    Check day-over-day and month-end changes, drawdowns, and monthly gains and losses.
